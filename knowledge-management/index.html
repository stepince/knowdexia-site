KNOWLEDGE MANAGEMENT

Make what you know easier to find again.

Knowledge management starts with keeping important material accessible. Knowdexia adds content retrieval and cited answers to a persistent document library.

Organize around your work

Use collections for a research area, a product, a project, or a set of reports. These groupings also become search scopes, so organization helps control which material informs a result.

Keep a library, not a temporary conversation

Uploaded documents and their indexes persist in the current local application. Add files as your work develops, move them into collections, and delete documents that are no longer useful.

Understand the current scope

Knowdexia currently focuses on uploaded documents, collections, retrieval, and source navigation. Shared team workspaces, hosted accounts, and external content connectors are not available in the inspected application.

Searchable knowledge stays useful when you can inspect its sources. Explore the interactive citation example.
