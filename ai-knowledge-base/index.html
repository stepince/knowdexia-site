AI KNOWLEDGE BASE

A knowledge base that helps you find answers.

A folder stores files. An AI knowledge base also makes their contents retrievable. Knowdexia brings your documents into a persistent library that you can search and question.

Start with the material you already have

Upload PDFs, Word documents, spreadsheets, Markdown, HTML, and plain text. Knowdexia extracts readable content, divides it into passages, and indexes those passages for retrieval. Image-only scans need text extraction before upload; OCR is not a current feature.

Give your library a useful shape

Group related material into collections, such as product documentation, research, or reports. You can move documents between collections and limit a search to the material relevant to your task.

Ask with evidence in reach

With an AI provider configured, Knowdexia uses retrieved passages to generate cited answers. Without a provider, it can return a cited extract. In either case, inspect the source before relying on an answer.

Searchable knowledge stays useful when you can inspect its sources. Explore the interactive citation example.
