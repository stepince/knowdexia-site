AI SECOND BRAIN

Keep the knowledge. Find the connection.

Your second brain should help you return to what you have learned. Knowdexia makes a collection of documents searchable, so useful material is easier to recover when a new question appears.

Collect deliberately

Bring in research, technical notes, reports, and other supported documents you want to revisit. Collections help keep a growing library organized around subjects that matter to you.

Retrieve without remembering the exact phrase

Ask a natural-language question or search for a concept. Semantic retrieval can help uncover related wording; keyword retrieval also helps with precise terms. The system searches the material you provide.

Use AI as a starting point for understanding

Generated answers require a configured AI provider and draw on retrieved passages. Citations give you a path back to those passages. Knowdexia does not currently create an automatic knowledge graph or extract a network of entities.

Searchable knowledge stays useful when you can inspect its sources. Explore the interactive citation example.
