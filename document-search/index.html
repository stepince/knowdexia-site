DOCUMENT SEARCH

Find the passage. Not just the file.

Finding a filename is only the beginning. Knowdexia searches the content inside supported documents and lets you open the passage that made a result relevant.

Search across different formats

Bring together text-based PDFs, DOCX, XLSX, XLS, CSV, Markdown, HTML, and TXT files. Supported formats share a retrieval workflow, so a question can surface a paragraph in a report alongside a relevant spreadsheet passage.

Read results in context

Each result carries a document name, a snippet, and available source location information. Depending on the format, that may include a page, heading, sheet, or row. The viewer helps you inspect the supporting passage.

Know the limits of extraction

Search quality depends on readable source text. Complex layouts and spreadsheet structure can affect extraction. Knowdexia does not currently perform OCR, so image-only PDFs need a text layer before they can be usefully searched.

Searchable knowledge stays useful when you can inspect its sources. Explore the interactive citation example.
